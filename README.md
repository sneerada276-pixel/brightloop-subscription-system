Overview
BrightLoop is a subscription management system concept designed to help businesses organize subscription plans and keep track of customer subscriptions in one place. The project aims to make subscription-related workflows easier to understand and manage.
Project status: Hackathon project. Update this README to match the features and technology actually implemented by your team.
Problem Statement
Managing subscriptions can become difficult when customer details, plans, renewal dates, and subscription statuses are tracked manually or across separate tools. This can lead to missed renewals, confusing records, and unnecessary administrative work.
Proposed Solution
BrightLoop aims to provide a centralized workflow for managing subscription information. Depending on the implementation, users may be able to review plans, associate customers with subscriptions, monitor subscription status, and track upcoming renewals.
